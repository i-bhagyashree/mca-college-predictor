Reference

MCA Admission Rules 2026-27

A plain-English summary of the official MP-DTE rule book (प्रवेश नियम 2026-27) for MCA. Page numbers refer to the PDF; if anything differs, the PDF is correct.

Official rule book (PDF)

Who can apply

  • Indian citizen.
  • Any bachelor's degree of at least 3 years: B.E./B.Tech, B.Sc, B.Com, B.A., B.Voc, BCA and others.
  • Maths at 10+2 or graduation level. Without a Maths background you must complete the university's bridge course.
  • At least 50% overall in graduation, or 45% for MP-domicile SC, ST and OBC (non-creamy layer).

§12.1, §12(2), p.25, 30–31

How merit is decided

  • MCA merit is based on your qualifying-exam marks (graduation %).
  • If seats are still vacant after Round 1, DTE may hold a second round on entrance-exam rank and/or qualifying marks.
  • In a round based on qualifying marks, DTE may treat all seats as unreserved and use one combined merit list.
  • A national-level sports gold medal earns 10% extra weightage.

§12(2), §13.1, §14.8, §16.3, p.31, 35, 38–39

Equal marks (tie-break)

  1. The older candidate ranks higher.
  2. If the age is also the same, names are ordered alphabetically (English).

§15.5, p.37

Types of seats

Government, aided & university colleges
  • 5% NRI (if approved), 5% All-India (AI).
  • The rest are MP-domicile seats. Vacant NRI/AI seats are added to them.
  • Other-state candidates get only seats left vacant in the final stage.
Private colleges
  • 5% NRI (if approved).
  • The rest are the General Pool, with reservation for MP SC/ST/OBC.
  • UR General-Pool seats are open to candidates from all states.

§7.1, §16.5, p.11, 39

For postgraduate courses, only the NCC, Divyang and Female horizontal quotas apply (Sainik, Freedom-Fighter and Technical-Stream quotas don't). You can claim only one reserved category.

§8.1, §8.2, §10.2, §10.3, p.14–17, 20–22

How seats are allotted

  • A reserved-category candidate high enough on the UR merit list is given a UR seat first, and a reserved seat only if no preferred UR seat is available.
  • Vacant reserved seats move between categories: ST→SC, SC→ST, SC/ST→OBC, OBC→ST. Anything still vacant becomes UR. This is what "After Conversion" means in the cut-off lists.
  • Vacant F seats go to open (OP) candidates of the same class.

§8.2 VI, §16.2, p.17, 38

Tuition Fee Waiver (TFW)

  • 5% extra seats per course, in AICTE colleges where at least 50% of seats were filled last year.
  • Family income no more than ₹8 lakh a year, and MP domicile only.
  • Waives the tuition fee only; you pay all other fees.
  • No change of college or course for the whole programme. Unfilled TFW seats are not given to others.

§10.2, p.20–21

After you get a seat

  • Upload the category/domicile certificates at registration and show originals when reporting. Originals are returned after checking, except TC and migration.
  • Pay the AFRC-fixed fee at the allotted college. Don't pay anyone else.
  • No transfer between colleges in the admission year.
  • No tuition-fee refund if you cancel after the refund date.
  • False information means the admission is cancelled at any time.

§9, §17.3–17.4, §18, p.18, 40–41

About this Project

Objective, methodology, data sources and technology behind the MCA College Predictor.

Minor Project · MCA III Semester

Department of Computer Applications, University Institute of Technology, RGPV, Bhopal

BS
Bhagyashree Soni0101CA251010

Objective

Every year thousands of students in Madhya Pradesh choose MCA colleges through MP-DTE online counselling. The information they need is spread across dozens of PDF cut-off lists, an intake portal, two fee portals and a 58-page rule book in Hindi. This project collects all of it automatically, cleans it, and presents it as one searchable, traceable tool, with a year-by-year test of whether machine learning can predict the next year better.

  • –cut-off PDFs processed
  • –cut-off rows extracted
  • –colleges matched to official IDs
  • –colleges with fee data

Methodology

  1. 1

    Collect

    Python scripts replay the MP-DTE, AFRC and DTE fee portal forms (ASP.NET postbacks) to download cut-off PDFs, the intake list, fee tables and the rule book. Raw files are kept unchanged with SHA-256 checksums.

  2. 2

    Extract

    pdfplumber reads tables from the PDF ruling lines. Rotated column headers are identified by their set of letters, so layout changes between years are detected, not silently mis-read.

  3. 3

    Clean & match

    College names are joined to official College IDs across ten years using exact normalised names plus a reviewed alias list. Categories are split into social group, seat class and gender.

  4. 4

    Validate

    Automated checks: no missing serial numbers, opening ≤ closing rank, valid codes, unmatched names written to review files, and 36 unit tests.

  5. 5

    Test ML

    Last year's cut-off, the average, a weighted average, random forest and gradient boosting (scikit-learn) were tested year by year on 2017–2026. ML gave only a small gain, so predictions use last year's cut-offs.

  6. 6

    Publish

    A static website (HTML, CSS, JavaScript) loads the generated data file. It works offline, needs no server, and shows the source PDF page for every number.

Limitations

  • This is an unofficial student project. Always confirm with the official MP-DTE and AFRC portals before making decisions.
  • Past cut-offs are guides, not guarantees. Machine learning was tested and did not predict reliably better (see ML Analysis).
  • The intake list for 2026-27 is tentative and may change before counselling.
  • The DTE fee page doesn't say whether its fee is per year or for the whole course.
