# ML predictor: hold-out test

Generated by `pipeline/evaluate_ml.py`. For each test year the model is trained only on earlier years and its predictions are compared with the real cut-offs of that year. *Historical* = last year's real cut-off, the method of the Historical Predictor page. Every number is computed, not estimated.

| Test year | Trained on | Seat pools | ML error (avg) | Historical error (avg) | ML median | Historical median | ML within ±100 | Historical within ±100 | ML closer in | Real cut-off inside ML range |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2024 | 2017–2023 | 129 | 307 | 334 | 198 | 234 | 28% | 35% | 60% of pools | 84% |
| 2025 | 2017–2024 | 89 | 138 | 166 | 76 | 83 | 55% | 55% | 62% of pools | 89% |
| 2026 | 2017–2025 | 104 | 180 | 182 | 113 | 97 | 47% | 50% | 64% of pools | 85% |

The likely range targets 80% (10th–90th percentile of past errors).

*Seat decision* = "you would get this seat" yes/no was right. *Exact label* = Safe/Likely/Reach/Unlikely was right. *Competitive* = colleges whose real cut-off closed before 90% of the list (open-seat colleges excluded).

## Verdict

- Averaged over 2024, 2025, 2026: ML cut-off error **208 ranks** vs Historical **228** (-8%). ML was closer for 62% of seat pools.
- Seat decision right: ML 85%, Historical 84%; on competitive colleges ML 82%, Historical 80%.
- Exact label right: ML 74%, Historical 74%.
- Likely range contained the real cut-off 86% of the time (target 80%).
